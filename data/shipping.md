# Meridian Supply — Shipping

## United States

| Service | Timing | Cost |
| ------- | ------ | ---- |
| Ground | 3–5 business days | Free over $55, otherwise $5.50 |
| Express | 1–2 business days | $13.50 |
| Overnight | Next business day | $26.00 (order by 12:00 ET) |

## International

Ships to 40+ countries in roughly 8–15 business days. Duties and taxes are
paid by the recipient at delivery.

## Processing

Orders before 12:00 ET on business days usually leave the same day. Weekend
orders ship the next business day. Peak holiday weeks may add 1–2 days.

## Lost packages

Domestic packages with no scan for 8 business days (or 18 internationally)
qualify for reshipment or refund — provide the order ID to support.

## Address locks

Address edits stop once the order moves to `shipped`.
