# Meridian Supply — Limited Warranty

## Coverage

Most Meridian hard goods include a **12-month** limited warranty against
manufacturing defects from the delivery date.

## Covered

- Seam or weld failures under normal use
- Defective buckles, zippers, or hardware on packs
- Meridian-branded accessory electronics that fail within the window

## Not covered

- Cosmetic wear, scratches, fading
- Misuse, unauthorized modifications, liquid damage (unless waterproof-rated)
- Final Sale and consumable products

## Claims

Send the order ID, a short description, and clear photos. Approved claims
receive a replacement or store credit. Visual review is handled by a human
agent via support ticket.
