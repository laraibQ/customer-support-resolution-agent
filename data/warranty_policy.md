# Meridian Supply — Warranty Policy

## Coverage

Most Meridian hard goods (bottles, packs, tools, electronics accessories)
include a **1-year limited warranty** covering manufacturing defects in
materials and workmanship from the delivery date.

## What is covered

- Cracks, seam failures, or hardware defects under normal use
- Defective zippers, buckles, or welded seams on bags
- Battery failures on Meridian-branded electronics accessories within
  the warranty window (excluding third-party batteries)

## What is not covered

- Normal wear and tear, cosmetic scratches, or fading
- Damage from misuse, drops, water immersion (unless product is rated
  waterproof), or unauthorized modifications
- Consumables and Final Sale items

## How to file a warranty claim

1. Provide the order ID and a short description of the defect.
2. Attach clear photos of the issue and the product serial/SKU label.
3. Support will open a human ticket for review within 2 business days.
4. Approved claims receive a replacement or store credit — no return of
   the defective unit is required unless requested.

## Warranty vs returns

- **Returns** (30-day window): buyer's remorse, wrong size, unused items.
- **Warranty**: manufacturing defects after the return window or for
  items that cannot be sold as new.
