# Meridian Supply — Returns & Refunds

## Window

- Unused standard items: **30 days** from delivery.
- Final Sale badges: non-returnable.
- Opened consumables: non-returnable.

## Condition

Return items unused, tagged, and in original packaging. We may deduct up to
15% if the item cannot be restocked.

## Steps

1. Orders → choose the order → Start return (or ask support to open one).
2. Choose a reason and download the prepaid label.
3. Drop off within 14 days of creating the return.

## Timing

- Refunds go to the original payment method.
- Cards: 5–10 business days after warehouse intake.
- PayPal: about 2–4 business days.
- Store credit: immediate after intake.

## Damaged or incorrect item

Photograph the issue within 7 days of delivery. Meridian ships a replacement
at no cost; a human review ticket is usually required for photos.
